Studio facts

PurposeLab Studio is an independent software studio based in India and serving people worldwide. It builds focused apps and browser tools for journaling, remembering, everyday routines, home health records, and caregiving.

The studio was founded in 2025 by developer and product designer Atul Chaturvedi. Its products are designed around a clear job, transparent limits, no advertising, and minimal data collection appropriate to how each product works.

PurposeLab is an online software business, not a local storefront or medical provider. Health-related products record or explain information and do not diagnose, treat, or replace professional care.

Product summaries

Zolio

A private daily journal and mood tracker for Android and iPhone. Zolio was formerly called Folio; the existing app identity, store listing, package identifier, and `/folio/` web addresses remain continuous.

Product facts and store links

Crumbs

A capture and recall service inside WhatsApp for notes, reminders, links, photos, and voice messages. Unlike the offline-first apps, Crumbs processes and stores messages on servers so it can answer retrieval requests.

Product facts and access details

WaterWise

An Android water logging and reminder app with one-tap logging, adjustable reminders, a home-screen widget, offline use, no account requirement, and no advertising.

Product facts and store link

BP Log

An Android app for recording readings from a separate home blood-pressure monitor, viewing trends, and preparing reports. It does not measure blood pressure and is not a medical device.

Product facts and store link

Hushly

An Android sleep-sound app for caregivers with offline playback, a timer, background controls, no account requirement, and no advertising.
