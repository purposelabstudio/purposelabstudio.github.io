Product choices

Journal and reflection

Zolio

For writing about your day, noticing moods and habits, and looking back with monthly reflections. Zolio is available on Android and iPhone. Your notebook is stored on your device by default; backup to your personal Google Drive or iCloud account is optional.

Explore Zolio Start a journaling guide
Capture and recall

Crumbs

For saving notes, ideas, reminders, links, photos, and voice messages where you already chat. Crumbs works inside WhatsApp, not as a separate mobile app. It runs on servers so it can receive and retrieve what you save; its product page explains the data-processing details.

Explore Crumbs See the capture workflow
Water routine

WaterWise

For logging water and setting reminders with one-tap logging and a home-screen widget. WaterWise is an Android app that works offline and does not require an account.

Explore WaterWise Estimate a daily target
Home BP record

BP Log

For recording readings from a home blood pressure monitor, reviewing trends, and preparing a PDF report to share with a doctor. BP Log is an Android app that keeps readings on your device. It does not measure blood pressure and is not a medical device.

Explore BP Log Check a reference category
Sleep sounds

Hushly

For starting sleep sounds with simple controls, a timer, and background playback. Hushly is an Android app with offline sound playback and no account required.
